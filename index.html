Gib Stopping & Finishing

Turn rough drywall into a flawless base for your perfect paint. Durable, smooth, and stunning.

  • Taping for Strength: Paper tape for long-lasting projects; mesh tape for quick jobs.
  • Perfect Angles: Internal corners hand-creased; external corners reinforced with beads.
